Delete your account

You can delete your Royal Marble account and its data at any time.

In the app

Open the menu → My profile → Delete my account, then confirm with your password (or your Google / Apple sign-in).

On this page

Sign in with the same account you use in the app, then confirm.

or

Signed in with Apple? Delete the account from the iPhone app, or email us (below).

Signed in as .

This permanently deletes your account. It can't be undone.

What is deleted

  • Your profile: name, email, phone number, nationality, company and role
  • Your profile photo
  • Your last known location and phone status
  • Your sign-in account

Deletion is immediate.

What is kept

Work records your employer needs — attendance (check-in and check-out times and sites), pay and payroll, and sales visit logs — are kept by Royal Marble as employment records and are no longer linked to an active account. Crash reports are deleted automatically after 90 days.

Can't sign in?

Email royalmarble.uae@gmail.com from the address you registered with, with the subject "Delete my account". We delete the account within 30 days.
